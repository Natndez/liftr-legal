      This explains what Liftr collects, why, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. Liftr doesn't sell your data, doesn't show ads, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. There are no analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

      Local first: your lifts are saved on your phone the moment you log them, then synced to our servers so they're backed up, available on your other devices, and visible to the people you share them with.

      What we collect

      Your account

      • Email address and password. To sign you in. Your password is stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see it.
      • Username, display name, bio and profile photo. These make up your profile, which other Liftr users can see.
      • When you agreed to the Terms of Use, and which version.

      What you tell us when you set up

      • Date of birth. Required at sign-up, to confirm you're 13 or older.
      • Sex, height and weight. Optional. Sex picks the body outline on your muscle map, weight is used for bodyweight milestones, and height is only shown back to you.
      • Units, experience level, training goal, how often you train, what equipment you have, and how you heard about Liftr. Optional.

      None of this is shown on your profile.

      Your training and social activity

      • Lifts: names, notes, exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, distances, and when you trained. Plus custom exercises and favorites. Templates stay on your phone.
      • Photos you add to lifts (up to three per lift).
      • Comments, comment likes, props and follows.
      • Each lift's privacy setting: "Anyone" or "Only me".
      • Reports you file and people you block.

      Phone number and contacts (optional)

      • Phone number. Only if you add one, only so people who have you in their contacts can find you on Liftr. It's stored privately: nobody can look it up or see it on your profile. You can change it in Account Options, and it's deleted with your account.
      • Contacts. Only if you tap Find Friends and allow access. Names never leave your phone. The phone numbers are converted to a standard format and sent to our server once, just to check which ones belong to Liftr users. They're not stored. We keep only a daily count of how many lookups you ran and how many numbers they included, to stop abuse. Contacts who aren't on Liftr can be invited through your phone's share sheet; we never message anyone.

      Notifications and devices

      • Push token. If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send pushes to your phone, and whether it's iOS or Android.
      • Your notification history in the app: who gave you props, commented, followed you, and so on.

      Server logs

      Like any online service, our hosting provider keeps standard logs of requests (such as IP address and time) for security and troubleshooting.

      Who can see what

      WhatWho
      Your profile (name, username, bio, photo), followers and who you followOther Liftr users, except anyone you've blocked or who has blocked you
      Lifts set to "Anyone", with their photos, props and commentsPeople who follow you and anyone who opens your profile, minus blocks

      Your comments and propsAnyone who can see the lift they're on
      Your name on leaderboards and community goalsOther Liftr users
      Date of birth, sex, height, weight, phone number, emailOnly you (and us, to run the service)

      A note on photos: to load quickly, photos are stored at long, unguessable web addresses. The app only shows those addresses to people who can see the lift, but anyone who already has a photo's address can open it.

      Why we use it

      • To run Liftr: sign you in, sync and back up your lifts, show your feed, profile, leaderboards and notifications.
      • To keep it safe: enforce the Terms of Use, review reports, act on abuse, and rate-limit lookups.
      • To support you when you contact us.

      We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

      Who processes it for us

      • Supabase: database, sign-in, photo storage and server functions. Almost everything above lives here.
      • Expo: relays push notifications to your device.
      • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): deliver those pushes to iPhones and Android phones.

      They handle data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

      How long we keep it

      As long as your account exists. Delete a comment or a photo and it's removed from our servers. Delete a lift and it disappears from the app everywhere straight away; its record is kept, hidden, until you delete your account. Delete your account and everything tied to it goes: your profile, lifts, photos, comments, props, follows, blocks, phone number, push tokens, notifications about you, and reports about you. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are overwritten on their normal schedule. Reports you filed about others are deleted with your account too.

      Your choices

      • Edit your profile any time in the app.
      • Make any lift private, or set new lifts to "Only me" by default in Settings.
      • Turn notifications off in your phone's Settings.
      • Skip contacts and your phone number entirely. Liftr works without them.
      • Block anyone, and report anything, from its "…" menu.
      • Get a copy of your data: email us and we'll send it.
      • Delete your account in the app: Settings, then Account Options, then Delete account. It takes effect immediately.

      Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your data, like correcting it or objecting to how it's used. Email us and we'll sort it out.

      Children

      Liftr is for people 13 and older. Sign-up asks for your date of birth and won't create an account for anyone younger. If we learn we have data from someone under 13, we delete it. If you think that's happened, email us.

      Security

      Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers, and database rules limit each account to the data it's allowed to see. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

      Changes

      If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
