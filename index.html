What's inside

Everything you need between sets. Nothing you don't.

Liftr records what you did and shows it back to you clearly. It doesn't tell you what to do next.

Log fast

Pick a split and go.

  • 200+ exercises, or make your own
  • Barbell, dumbbell, bodyweight, timed holds, reps only, and sleds and carries by distance
  • Rest timer, plus a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Save templates, run any past lift again, or backfill one you forgot to log

See your work

Your history, laid out properly.

  • PRs picked up automatically, every one on your PR wall
  • Estimated 1RM, progress per exercise, and your compound total
  • A volume map of where your sets went, muscle by muscle
  • Weekly streaks, a training heatmap, and trophies from your own history

Lift with friends

A feed of the people you actually train with.

  • Give props, comment and reply
  • Up to three photos on any lift
  • Find friends from your contacts, if you want to
  • Monthly leaderboard seasons and weekly community goals

Your call

You decide who sees what.

  • Any lift can be public or Only me
  • Make private the default for new lifts
  • Block or report anyone, from any lift, comment or profile
  • Delete your account and everything in it, any time

Privacy

Your lifts, your call.

Liftr doesn't sell your data, doesn't show ads, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. There are no analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

Private lifts

Set any lift to Only me. It stays off the feed and your profile, and still counts toward your totals.

Safe by default

Block or report from any "…" menu. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Gone when you say so

Delete your account in the app and your lifts, photos and comments go with it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else, email dev.nathanf@gmail.com.

Is Liftr free?

Yes. There are no ads, and nothing in the app costs money.

What phones does it run on?

iPhone, on iOS 15.1 or later. Android isn't out yet.

What's TestFlight?

Apple's own app for trying apps before they're on the App Store. Liftr is in beta there right now. Your lifts are saved to your account, so they come with you when Liftr moves to the App Store.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Every lift saves to your phone the moment you log it, then syncs when you're back online. Basement gyms welcome.

Who can see my lifts?

Lifts set to Anyone show up for people who follow you and anyone who opens your profile. Lifts set to Only me are just for you. You can switch any lift either way, and make Only me the default. The privacy policy has the full breakdown.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the "…" on any lift, comment or profile. Blocking hides the two of you from each other straight away. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, then Account Options, then Delete account. It takes effect immediately and removes everything tied to your account.
